## Topic 39 – Gaganyaan — India's Human Spaceflight Programme

### Questions for Topic 39

### Question 1
What does "Gaganyaan" mean?

A) Star Vehicle
B) Sky Vehicle
C) Moon Ship
D) Space Station

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Gaganyaan means "Sky Vehicle" in Sanskrit (Gagan = sky, Yaan = vehicle). It is India's ambitious human spaceflight programme aimed at sending Indian astronauts to low Earth orbit and returning them safely, making India the fourth country to independently launch humans into space.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 2
What is the Indian term for astronauts in the Gaganyaan programme?

A) Taikonaut
B) Cosmonaut
C) Vyomanaut
D) Gaganaut

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Indian astronauts are called Vyomanauts (Vyom means "sky" or "space" in Sanskrit). This follows the tradition of different spacefaring nations having their own terms: astronaut (USA), cosmonaut (Russia), taikonaut (China), and spationaut (France).

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 3
How many crew members will Gaganyaan's crewed mission carry?

A) 1
B) 2
C) 3
D) 6

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Gaganyaan is designed to carry a 3-member crew to low Earth orbit at approximately 400 km altitude. The crew will spend up to 3 days in orbit before returning to Earth via splashdown in the Bay of Bengal or Arabian Sea, where the Indian Navy will recover them.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 4
If Gaganyaan succeeds, India will become the ___ country to independently launch humans into space.

A) Second
B) Third
C) Fourth
D) Fifth

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** India will become the fourth country to independently launch humans into space using its own spacecraft and rocket, after Russia (Vostok 1, 1961), the USA (Mercury/Freedom 7, 1961), and China (Shenzhou 5, 2003). A common mistake is saying "third" — China has been launching taikonauts since 2003.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO / Space History

### Question 5
Which launch vehicle is being used for Gaganyaan?

A) PSLV
B) GSLV Mk II
C) Human-rated LVM3 (HLVM3)
D) SSLV

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Gaganyaan uses the Human-rated LVM3 (HLVM3) — a specially modified version of LVM3 (formerly GSLV Mk III). "Human-rating" means the rocket has been enhanced with additional safety features and reliability margins, including a Crew Escape System (CES) that can pull the crew away from the rocket in case of a failure.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 6
What is the Crew Escape System (CES)?

A) An ejection seat like in fighter jets
B) Quick-firing solid rocket motors that pull the crew module away from the rocket during an emergency
C) A backup parachute system
D) An emergency oxygen supply

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Crew Escape System consists of solid rocket motors mounted atop the crew module that can rapidly fire and pull the capsule away from the launch vehicle in case of a failure. The CES must work at any point during launch — from the pad through the entire ascent phase. It is essential for crew safety and has been tested in both the Pad Abort Test and TV-D1.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 7
When was the Gaganyaan programme announced?

A) 26 January 2015
B) 15 August 2018
C) 14 July 2023
D) 2 October 2019

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Gaganyaan programme was announced by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Independence Day, 15 August 2018, during his address from the Red Fort. He declared that India would send its astronauts to space by 2022 (the 75th anniversary of independence). The timeline has since shifted due to COVID-19 and technical requirements.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** Government of India / ISRO

### Question 8
What was the Pad Abort Test, and when was it conducted?

A) A test of the launch vehicle's engines on 15 August 2018
B) A successful test of the Crew Escape System from the launch pad on 5 July 2018
C) A test of the parachute system on 21 October 2023
D) A cancelled test in 2020

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Pad Abort Test was conducted on 5 July 2018 and successfully demonstrated the Crew Escape System. A test capsule was propelled away from the launch pad by CES motors, reached approximately 2.7 km altitude, and was recovered from the sea. This proved that the CES could save crew during a launch pad emergency.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 9
What was TV-D1 and when was it conducted?

A) A television broadcast of the Gaganyaan mission
B) The first in-flight test of the abort system during ascent, conducted on 21 October 2023
C) A test drive of the crew transport vehicle
D) A full crewed mission test in 2024

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** TV-D1 (Test Vehicle Demonstration 1), conducted on 21 October 2023, was the first integrated test of the Crew Escape System during actual flight. A single-stage liquid rocket launched, and at approximately 12 km altitude, the CES activated and pulled the crew module away. The module deployed parachutes and splashed down in the Bay of Bengal, where it was recovered by the Indian Navy.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 10
Who or what is Vyommitra?

A) An Indian astronaut
B) A half-humanoid robot that will fly on uncrewed Gaganyaan test missions
C) The name of the Gaganyaan crew capsule
D) ISRO's mission control AI system

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Vyommitra ("Space Friend" — Vyom = space, Mitra = friend) is a half-humanoid robot developed by ISRO. It is a female-presenting humanoid with head, torso, and arms (but no legs, as it sits in the crew module). Vyommitra will fly on uncrewed Gaganyaan missions to test life support systems, simulate crew activities, and monitor the cabin environment before humans fly.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 11
Why is Vyommitra described as a "half-humanoid"?

A) It only has one arm
B) It has only an upper body (head, torso, arms) but no legs
C) It is half the size of a human
D) It can only perform half the tasks a human can

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Vyommitra is called a half-humanoid because it has only an upper body — head, torso, and arms, but no legs. This design makes sense because the robot needs to sit in the crew module seat and operate switches and panels, tasks that don't require legs. It can perform switch operations, respond to voice commands, and simulate crew activities.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 12
Where were the Gaganyaan astronaut candidates initially trained?

A) NASA Johnson Space Centre, USA
B) ESA Astronaut Centre, Germany
C) Yuri Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Centre, Star City, Russia
D) ISRO Satellite Centre, Bengaluru

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The four selected Gaganyaan astronaut candidates (all Indian Air Force test pilots) underwent initial training at the Yuri Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Centre in Star City, Russia. Russia has decades of experience training cosmonauts and international astronauts. Advanced training continues at the Astronaut Training Facility in Bengaluru.

### Question 13
Who is Shubhanshu Shukla in the context of Gaganyaan?

A) The ISRO Chairman who oversees Gaganyaan
B) A Gaganyaan astronaut candidate who became the first to go to space via the ISS
C) The engineer who designed the crew capsule
D) The pilot of the first Gaganyaan mission

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla is one of the four Gaganyaan astronaut candidates. He became the first Gaganyaan candidate to go to space when he flew aboard the NASA SpaceX Axiom-4 mission to the International Space Station in 2025. This gave India valuable spaceflight experience ahead of the indigenous Gaganyaan missions.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO / NASA

### Question 14
How will the Gaganyaan crew return to Earth?

A) Landing on a runway like a space shuttle
B) Splashdown in the sea (Bay of Bengal or Arabian Sea) with parachute recovery by the Indian Navy
C) Landing in a desert with airbag cushioning
D) Docking at a space station and transferring to another vehicle

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Gaganyaan crew module will re-enter Earth's atmosphere protected by an ablative heat shield, deploy parachutes to slow down, and splash down in the Bay of Bengal or Arabian Sea. The Indian Navy will conduct the sea recovery operation. This capsule-based splashdown approach is similar to NASA's Apollo and SpaceX Crew Dragon missions.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 15
What anomaly occurred during the TV-D1 test?

A) The rocket engines failed to ignite
B) One of the drogue parachute deployment devices did not fire as expected
C) The crew module sank after splashdown
D) The abort system failed to activate

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** During TV-D1, one of the drogue parachute deployment devices did not fire as expected. However, the system still functioned adequately with the remaining parachute(s), and the crew module successfully splashed down and was recovered. This is exactly why test flights are conducted — to identify and fix issues before humans fly.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 16
What is the planned sequence of Gaganyaan missions?

A) One single crewed mission
B) G1 (uncrewed with Vyommitra), G2 (uncrewed dress rehearsal), H1 (first crewed mission)
C) Three crewed missions back-to-back
D) One uncrewed followed immediately by a crewed mission

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Gaganyaan follows a careful, phased approach: G1 sends the crew module with Vyommitra to orbit and back (testing all systems uncrewed), G2 is a full dress rehearsal (also uncrewed with Vyommitra), and H1 is the first crewed mission carrying 3 vyomanauts to LEO for up to 3 days. This step-by-step approach prioritises crew safety.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 17
What is the approximate combined mass of the Gaganyaan Crew Module and Service Module?

A) About 3,000 kg
B) About 5,500 kg
C) About 11,000 kg
D) About 20,000 kg

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The Crew Module weighs approximately 8,200 kg and the Service Module approximately 2,880 kg, for a combined total of about 11,000 kg. This is the heaviest payload India has launched, requiring the full capability of the human-rated LVM3. The Service Module provides propulsion, power (solar panels), and life support during the orbital phase.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 18
What was the first Indian in space, and how does this relate to Gaganyaan?

A) Shubhanshu Shukla flew on Gaganyaan in 2025
B) Rakesh Sharma flew on a Soviet Soyuz in 1984; Gaganyaan will be India's first indigenous human spaceflight
C) Kalpana Chawla flew on Gaganyaan in 2003

**Explanation:** Wing Commander Rakesh Sharma became the first Indian in space on 3 April 1984, flying aboard the Soviet Soyuz T-11 spacecraft to the Salyut 7 space station. However, this was on a Soviet vehicle, not an Indian one. Gaganyaan aims to make India capable of independently sending its own astronauts to space using Indian rockets and spacecraft.

### Question 20
At what approximate altitude will Gaganyaan orbit Earth?

A) 100 km
B) 200 km
C) 400 km
D) 36,000 km

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Gaganyaan's crew mission will orbit at approximately 400 km altitude — similar to the International Space Station's orbit. This is in the low Earth orbit (LEO) regime, well above the atmosphere but close enough to Earth for a quick return. The crew will spend up to 3 days at this altitude before re-entering the atmosphere.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 21
What is the Crew Module's heat shield for?

A) Protecting against solar radiation during orbit
B) Protecting the capsule from extreme heat during re-entry into Earth's atmosphere
C) Keeping the capsule warm during the cold of space
D) Protecting against micrometeorite impacts

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** When the Crew Module re-enters Earth's atmosphere at very high speed (approximately 7-8 km/s), friction with the air generates extreme temperatures — several thousand degrees Celsius on the heat shield surface. The ablative heat shield absorbs and dissipates this heat by gradually burning away, protecting the crew inside from the inferno outside.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 22
Which statement about Gaganyaan is FALSE?

A) Gaganyaan has already sent humans to space
B) The programme uses a human-rated version of LVM3
C) Vyommitra will fly on uncrewed test missions
D) Recovery will be by splashdown in the sea

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** As of 2026, Gaganyaan has NOT yet sent humans to space. Only test flights have occurred — the Pad Abort Test (2018) and TV-D1 (2023). The crewed mission (H1) has not yet launched. All other statements are correct: HLVM3 is the launch vehicle, Vyommitra will fly on G1/G2, and recovery is by sea splashdown.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO
