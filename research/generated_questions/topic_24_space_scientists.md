## Topic 24 – Key Indian Space Scientists and Leaders

### Questions for Topic 24

### Question 1
Who was the first Chairman of ISRO?

A) Homi Bhabha
B) Satish Dhawan
C) Vikram Sarabhai
D) APJ Abdul Kalam

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Vikram Sarabhai was the first Chairman of ISRO (and its predecessor INCOSPAR). He established INCOSPAR in 1962 and it became ISRO on August 15, 1969. Sarabhai's vision of using space technology for national development continues to guide ISRO today.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 2
Who is known as the "Missile Man of India"?

A) Vikram Sarabhai
B) Satish Dhawan
C) APJ Abdul Kalam
D) U R Rao

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** APJ Abdul Kalam earned the title "Missile Man of India" for leading the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), which developed missiles such as Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Trishul, and Nag. Before his missile work, Kalam was the Project Director of SLV-3 at ISRO.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** DRDO / Indian Space History

### Question 3
Who was the first Indian citizen to travel to space?

A) Kalpana Chawla
B) Sunita Williams
C) Rakesh Sharma
D) APJ Abdul Kalam

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Rakesh Sharma became the first (and so far only) Indian citizen to travel to space on April 3, 1984. He flew on the Soviet Soyuz T-11 mission to the Salyut 7 space station, spending about 7 days in space. Note that Kalpana Chawla and Sunita Williams are American citizens of Indian origin.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** Indian Space History

### Question 4
When asked by Prime Minister Indira Gandhi how India looked from space, what did Rakesh Sharma famously reply?

A) "Incredible India"
B) "Saare Jahan Se Achha" (the best in the world)
C) "Jai Hind"
D) "Like a blue marble"

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Rakesh Sharma's famous reply "Saare Jahan Se Achha" (meaning "better than the whole world") is from a patriotic poem/song written by Allama Iqbal. This exchange became one of the most iconic moments in Indian space history and is widely remembered and quoted.

**Difficulty:** Easy

### Question 5
Which ISRO centre is named after Satish Dhawan?

A) URSC in Bengaluru
B) VSSC in Thiruvananthapuram
C) SDSC SHAR in Sriharikota
D) PRL in Ahmedabad

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR (SDSC SHAR) at Sriharikota, India's primary spaceport, was renamed after Satish Dhawan in 2002 following his death. Dhawan was ISRO's second full-term Chairman (1972–1984) and is called the "Father of Experimental Fluid Dynamics in India."

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 6
Satish Dhawan is celebrated for a famous leadership principle. What was it?

A) He always took credit for both success and failure
B) When SLV-3 failed, he took the blame; when it succeeded, he gave the credit to APJ Abdul Kalam and the team
C) He refused to speak to the press under any circumstances
D) He only celebrated successes and ignored failures

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** When SLV-3's first experimental flight (E1, August 1979) failed, Dhawan personally faced the press and took full responsibility. When the second flight (E2, July 18, 1980) succeeded, he pushed APJ Abdul Kalam — the project director — to face the press and receive the credit. This "take blame for failure, give credit for success" principle became legendary in Indian space history and management studies.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO History / Leadership Studies

### Question 7
Prof. U R Rao is primarily known for building which aspect of India's space capability?

A) India's launch vehicle programme
B) India's satellite programme
C) India's human spaceflight programme
D) India's missile programme

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Prof. Udupi Ramachandra Rao (U R Rao) built India's satellite programme. As a young scientist, he was the project director of Aryabhata (India's first satellite, 1975). As ISRO Chairman (1984–1994), he expanded the INSAT communication satellite system and established the IRS remote sensing programme. ISAC was renamed U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC) in his honour in 2018.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

B) SDSC SHAR in Sriharikota

### Question 9
APJ Abdul Kalam served as what role in India's government?

A) Prime Minister of India
B) Chief Justice of India
C) 11th President of India

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** APJ Abdul Kalam served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Known as the "People's President," he was beloved for his simplicity and outreach to young people. Before his presidency, he had a distinguished career at ISRO (SLV-3 project director) and DRDO (missile development). He received India's highest civilian honour, the Bharat Ratna, in 1997.

### Question 10
What was APJ Abdul Kalam's role in ISRO?

A) ISRO Chairman
B) Project Director of the SLV-3 programme
C) Project Director of Chandrayaan-1
D) Designer of PSLV

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** APJ Abdul Kalam was the Project Director of India's first satellite launch vehicle, SLV-3. Under his technical leadership, SLV-3 successfully launched the Rohini RS-1 satellite on July 18, 1980, making India the 6th nation with independent satellite launch capability. Kalam was never ISRO Chairman — he moved to DRDO after SLV-3.

### Question 11
Which ISRO Chairman oversaw the successful Mars Orbiter Mission (MOM)?

A) K. Kasturirangan
B) G. Madhavan Nair
C) K. Radhakrishnan
D) K. Sivan

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** K. Radhakrishnan was ISRO Chairman from 2009 to 2014 and oversaw the Mars Orbiter Mission from concept to launch. MOM was launched on November 5, 2013 and entered Mars orbit on September 24, 2014 — just two days after Radhakrishnan's term ended. He also oversaw the GSLV-D5 success with India's indigenous cryogenic engine in January 2014.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 12
Kalpana Chawla was the first woman of Indian origin to go to space. How did she die?

A) During a spacewalk accident
B) In the Space Shuttle Columbia disaster during re-entry on February 1, 2003
C) In a training accident at NASA
D) She survived all her missions

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Kalpana Chawla died on February 1, 2003, when Space Shuttle Columbia broke apart during re-entry into Earth's atmosphere on mission STS-107. All seven crew members were killed. Chawla, born in Karnal, Haryana, had first flown in space on STS-87 in 1997. India honoured her memory by naming the Kalpana-1 meteorological satellite after her.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** NASA / Indian Space History

### Question 14

A) K. Sivan
B) S. Somanath

### Question 15

### Question 17

A) Vikram Sarabhai
B) APJ Abdul Kalam

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Prof. U R Rao was the project director of Aryabhata, India's first satellite, launched on April 19, 1975. ### Question 18

A) He was ISRO's first Chairman
B) He launched India's first rocket
C) He supported Sarabhai's space vision; INCOSPAR was initially under his Department of Atomic Energy
D) He designed India's first satellite

**Explanation:** Homi Jehangir Bhabha, known as the "Father of the Indian Nuclear/Atomic Energy Programme," supported Vikram Sarabhai's vision for a space programme. Bhabha died in a plane crash in 1966.
