## Topic 25 – Indian Launch Vehicles — PSLV

### Questions for Topic 25

### Question 1
What is the PSLV commonly called because of its exceptional reliability and versatility?

A) The Pride of India
B) The Workhorse of ISRO
C) The Moon Rocket
D) The Star Launcher

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** PSLV is called the "Workhorse of ISRO" because it is ISRO's most reliable and versatile launch vehicle with over 50 successful missions. It has launched satellites to various orbits — SSO, LEO, GTO, and even interplanetary trajectories — making it the backbone of India's launch capability.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 2
What does PSLV stand for?

A) Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle
B) Payload Satellite Lifting Vehicle
C) Primary Space Launch Vehicle
D) Propulsion System Launch Vehicle

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** PSLV stands for Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle. It was originally designed to launch satellites into Sun-Synchronous (polar) Orbits (SSO), which is why it has "Polar" in its name. However, despite the name, PSLV is capable of launching to many different orbit types.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 3
When was the first successful PSLV flight, and what satellite did it carry?

A) PSLV-D1 in 1993 carrying Rohini RS-1
B) PSLV-D2 in 1994 carrying IRS-P2
C) PSLV-C1 in 1999 carrying Oceansat-1
D) PSLV-C11 in 2008 carrying Chandrayaan-1

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The first successful PSLV flight was PSLV-D2 on October 15, 1994, which launched the IRS-P2 remote sensing satellite. The first PSLV flight (PSLV-D1 in September 1993) had failed due to an attitude control problem in the second stage. PSLV-D2's success marked the beginning of an extraordinary track record.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 4
How many stages does the PSLV have, and what is their propellant sequence?

A) Two stages: solid, then liquid
B) Three stages: solid, liquid, cryogenic
C) Four stages: solid, liquid, solid, liquid (alternating)
D) Four stages: liquid, solid, liquid, cryogenic

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV has an innovative four-stage design with alternating solid and liquid propellant stages: Stage 1 (solid), Stage 2 (liquid), Stage 3 (solid), Stage 4 (liquid). This alternating design was chosen to optimize performance — solid stages provide high thrust for initial acceleration, while liquid stages provide precise, controllable thrust for orbit insertion.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 5
What is the name of the large solid rocket motor used in PSLV's first stage?

A) Vikas
B) CE-7.5
C) S139
D) S200

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV's first stage uses the S139 solid rocket motor, which carries 139 tonnes of HTPB (Hydroxyl-Terminated Polybutadiene) propellant. The S139 is one of the largest solid rocket motors in the world. It burns for approximately 105 seconds, providing the massive thrust needed for liftoff. The S200 is the larger motor used on LVM3, not PSLV.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO / VSSC

### Question 6
Which engine powers the second stage of PSLV?

A) CE-20 cryogenic engine
B) Vikas engine
C) KVD-1 engine
D) RD-180 engine

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Vikas engine powers PSLV's liquid second stage (PS2). It was derived from the French Viking engine and is one of ISRO's most important propulsion systems. The Vikas engine uses UDMH (Unsymmetrical Dimethylhydrazine) and N₂O₄ (nitrogen tetroxide) as propellants and provides controlled, throttleable thrust for the ascent phase.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO / LPSC

### Question 7
What is special about PSLV's fourth stage (PS4)?

A) It uses cryogenic propellant
B) It has two restartable engines for precise orbit insertion
C) It is the largest stage on the vehicle
D) It uses nuclear propulsion

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** PSLV's fourth stage (PS4) features two restartable liquid engines that use MMH (Monomethylhydrazine) and MON-3 as propellants. The ability to restart multiple times is crucial because it allows precise orbit insertion — the engines can fire, coast, and fire again to adjust the satellite's final orbit. PS4 carries about 2.5 tonnes of propellant.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 8
Which PSLV variant is the most commonly used for major missions?

A) PSLV-G (Generic)
B) PSLV-CA (Core Alone)
C) PSLV-XL (eXtra Large)
D) PSLV-DL (Dual)

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV-XL is the most commonly used variant, featuring six extended PSOM-XL strap-on boosters with 12.2 tonnes of propellant each. It can carry approximately 1,750 kg to Sun-Synchronous Orbit. Most of ISRO's high-profile missions — including Chandrayaan-1, Mars Orbiter Mission, and Aditya-L1 — used the PSLV-XL variant.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 9
How does the PSLV-CA variant differ from the PSLV-XL?

A) PSLV-CA has more strap-on boosters
B) PSLV-CA has no strap-on boosters at all
C) PSLV-CA uses cryogenic engines
D) PSLV-CA is taller than PSLV-XL

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** PSLV-CA stands for "Core Alone" — it has no strap-on boosters at all, using only the four core stages. This makes it lighter and simpler, suitable for smaller payloads. In contrast, PSLV-XL has six large PSOM-XL strap-on boosters. The CA variant can still carry about 1,100 kg to LEO despite having no strap-ons.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 10
How many PSLV variants exist based on strap-on booster configurations?

A) Two (with and without boosters)
B) Three (G, CA, XL)
C) Five (G, CA, XL, DL, QL)
D) Seven different variants

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV has five variants: PSLV-G (Generic, 6 standard strap-ons), PSLV-CA (Core Alone, no strap-ons), PSLV-XL (eXtra Large, 6 enhanced PSOM-XL strap-ons), PSLV-DL (Dual, 2 PSOM-XL strap-ons), and PSLV-QL (Quad, 4 PSOM-XL strap-ons). This range of configurations gives PSLV the flexibility to match different payload requirements.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 12
Which PSLV mission launched Chandrayaan-1, India's first Moon mission?

A) PSLV-C7
B) PSLV-C11
C) PSLV-C25
D) PSLV-C37

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** PSLV-C11 launched Chandrayaan-1 on October 22, 2008, sending India's first spacecraft to the Moon. The PSLV-XL variant was used for this mission. Chandrayaan-1 orbited the Moon and its Moon Impact Probe (MIP) discovered water molecules on the lunar surface — one of the most significant discoveries in planetary science.

### Question 13
Which PSLV mission launched India's Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan)?

A) PSLV-C11
B) PSLV-C20
C) PSLV-C25
D) PSLV-C37

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV-C25 launched the Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) on November 5, 2013. Using the PSLV-XL variant, this mission sent India's first spacecraft to Mars, making India the first Asian nation and the first country to succeed in its maiden Mars mission attempt. The mission was also notable for its remarkably low cost (approximately ₹450 crore).

### Question 14
What is POEM, and how is it related to PSLV?

A) A planned new rocket to replace PSLV
B) A ground-based mission control system
C) The spent PS4 stage repurposed as an orbital experimental platform
D) A satellite navigation system

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** POEM (PSLV Orbital Experimental Module) is an innovative concept where PSLV's spent fourth stage (PS4) is repurposed as an orbital platform for scientific experiments. After deploying the main payload, the PS4 stage uses its residual fuel to orient itself and provides power via solar panels. First used on PSLV-C53 (2022), POEM turns what would be space debris into a useful orbital laboratory.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 15
What propellant does the PSLV first stage (S139) solid motor use?

A) Liquid hydrogen and liquid oxygen
B) UDMH and N₂O₄
C) HTPB (Hydroxyl-Terminated Polybutadiene)
D) Kerosene and liquid oxygen

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The S139 solid rocket motor uses HTPB (Hydroxyl-Terminated Polybutadiene) as its solid propellant, carrying 139 tonnes of it. HTPB is a rubber-like polymer that serves as both fuel and binder in the propellant mixture (combined with an oxidizer like ammonium perchlorate). It is widely used in solid rocket motors worldwide because of its good performance and handling characteristics.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO / VSSC

### Question 16
Which organisation developed the PSLV?

A) HAL (Hindustan Aeronautics Limited)
B) LPSC (Liquid Propulsion Systems Centre)
C) VSSC (Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre), Thiruvananthapuram
D) URSC (U R Rao Satellite Centre), Bengaluru

While LPSC contributed the liquid propulsion systems and URSC builds the satellite payloads, the overall vehicle design and integration is VSSC's responsibility.

### Question 17
What happened during the first PSLV flight (PSLV-D1)?

A) Complete success — launched IRS-P2
B) Failed due to an attitude control problem in the second stage
C) Failed due to a cryogenic engine malfunction
D) Was cancelled before launch

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** PSLV-D1, launched on September 20, 1993, was the first flight of the PSLV but it failed. The failure was caused by an attitude control problem during the second stage burn, which caused the vehicle to deviate from its planned trajectory. The satellite could not be placed in orbit. ISRO learned from this failure, and the next flight (PSLV-D2) was successful.

### Question 18
Despite being called a "Polar" Satellite Launch Vehicle, PSLV can launch satellites to:

A) Only polar orbits
B) Only Sun-synchronous orbits
C) Many different orbits including SSO, LEO, GTO, and interplanetary trajectories
D) Only Low Earth Orbit

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Despite the "Polar" in its name, PSLV is remarkably versatile and can launch to many orbit types: Sun-Synchronous Orbit (SSO), Low Earth Orbit (LEO), Geostationary Transfer Orbit (GTO), and even lunar and interplanetary transfer orbits. It launched Chandrayaan-1 to the Moon and Mangalyaan to Mars. The name reflects its original design purpose, not its actual capability.

### Question 20
Does PSLV use a cryogenic engine in any of its stages?

A) Yes, in the upper stage
B) Yes, in the first stage
C) No — PSLV uses solid and storable liquid propellants only
D) Yes, in the strap-on boosters

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV does NOT use any cryogenic engines. All four stages use either solid propellant (stages 1 and 3) or storable liquid propellants (stages 2 and 4). Cryogenic engines (which use ultra-cold liquid hydrogen and liquid oxygen) are used in GSLV (CE-7.5 engine) and LVM3 (CE-20 engine), not in PSLV. This is a common point of confusion.

**Difficulty:** Medium

**Source:** ISRO

### Question 21
PSLV-C57 launched which important solar observation mission in September 2023?

A) Chandrayaan-3
B) Mangalyaan-2

D) XPoSat

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV-C57 launched Aditya-L1, India's first solar observation mission, on September 2, 2023. Aditya-L1 was placed in a halo orbit around the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 1 (L1), about 1.5 million km from Earth, from where it continuously observes the Sun. This demonstrated PSLV's capability to inject payloads into complex trajectories far beyond Earth orbit.

### Question 23
Which PSLV mission had a partial failure in 2017 apart from the inaugural PSLV-D1?

A) PSLV-C25
B) PSLV-C37
C) PSLV-C39
D) PSLV-C40

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** PSLV-C39 (August 31, 2017) experienced a partial failure due to a payload fairing separation issue — the heat shield that protects the satellite during ascent did not separate as planned. This prevented the IRNSS-1H navigation satellite from being deployed correctly. Apart from PSLV-D1 (1993) and this incident, PSLV has maintained an outstanding success record.

### Question 24

### Question 27
The alternating solid-liquid-solid-liquid stage design of PSLV provides what key advantage?

A) It makes the rocket lighter
B) Solid stages give high thrust for acceleration while liquid stages give precise control
C) It eliminates the need for strap-on boosters
D) It allows cryogenic propulsion

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** PSLV's alternating design combines the strengths of both propulsion types. Solid rocket stages provide high thrust and are simpler and cheaper — ideal for the powerful first stage burn and upper atmosphere boost. Liquid stages offer precise control, throttling capability, and restartability — essential for controlled ascent and final orbit insertion. This hybrid approach optimizes overall mission performance.

### Question 32
What is the significance of the Vikas engine in India's space programme?

### Question 33
How does POEM contribute to reducing space debris?

A) It deorbits immediately after payload deployment
B) It repurposes the spent PS4 stage as a useful platform instead of leaving it as debris
