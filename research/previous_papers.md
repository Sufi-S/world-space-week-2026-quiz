## URSC World Space Week Event History (from ursc.gov.in)

URSC has organised WSW events every year from 2019 to 2025. Key findings:

### WSW 2025 (Theme: "Living in Space")
- **Format**: AIR FM Rainbow radio quiz (daily, 3-4 PM, Oct 4-9) + in-person competitions at 10 institutions across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Maharashtra
- **Participants**: ~12,000 students, 400 teachers, 250+ schools
- **Quiz style**: Questions on "space science, technology, space scientists" — first two correct answers per question won mementos
- **No quiz papers available online**

### WSW 2024 (Theme: "Space and Climate Change")
- **Format**: AIR FM Rainbow radio quiz (daily, 3-4 PM, 7 days) + live quiz at URSC with ~300 students
- **Participation**: ~1,000 WhatsApp entries/day for radio quiz
- **Outreach**: 9 institutions across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra
- **No quiz papers available online**

### WSW 2023 (Theme: "Space and Entrepreneurship")
- **Format**: AIR FM Rainbow radio quiz + in-person quiz at URSC (~600 students total)
- **Outreach**: 8 institutions across Karnataka
- **Mementos**: Featured Chandrayaan-3 lander image
- **No quiz papers available online**

### Pattern Observed
- URSC conducts Quiz + Memory + Elocution/Pick-and-Speak + Drawing/Painting competitions
- Quizzes cover "space science, technology, space scientists"
- Radio quizzes use WhatsApp for answers — suggests rapid-fire individual questions, not written papers
- In-person quizzes at schools appear to be team-based buzzer format
- Winners receive certificates, science books, and mementos
- No question papers have been published online in any year examined (2019-2025)

## Common Question Patterns (Inferred from Event Context)

Based on the URSC event structure and target audience (high school, Classes 6-10):

### Likely Question Categories
1. **ISRO missions** — dates, names, achievements, launch vehicles (HIGH frequency — this is an ISRO event)
2. **Indian space scientists** — Vikram Sarabhai, Satish Dhawan, Kalam, current ISRO chairman
3. **Indian satellites** — INSAT, IRS, NavIC, Chandrayaan, MOM, AstroSat, Aditya-L1
4. **Launch vehicles** — PSLV, GSLV, LVM3, SSLV stages and capabilities
5. **Solar system basics** — planets, Sun, Moon, basic facts
6. **International milestones** — first human in space, first Moon landing, ISS
7. **Current theme** — "Rocket Revolution" (expect questions on rocket history, types, pioneers)

### Expected Difficulty Distribution
- **Easy (50%)**: Direct factual recall (e.g., "Which was India's first satellite?")
- **Medium (35%)**: Requires understanding (e.g., "Why is the PSLV called the workhorse of ISRO?")
- **Hard (15%)**: Comparative/analytical (e.g., "How does cryogenic propulsion differ from solid propulsion?")

### Question Format
- Multiple Choice Questions (4 options) for written rounds
- Rapid-fire verbal questions for buzzer rounds
- Visual rounds (identify satellite/rocket from image)
