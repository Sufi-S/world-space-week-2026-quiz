## Verified Facts

### ISRO Chairman
- **V. Narayanan** confirmed as current ISRO Chairman (as of 2026)

### SpaDeX — Confirmed Successful
- Launched: 30 December 2024 (PSLV-C60)
- **Docking achieved: 16 January 2025** — confirmed successful
- India demonstrated autonomous orbital docking with indigenous technology
- Undocking and power transfer also successfully demonstrated
- Topics 40 data is CORRECT

### Gaganyaan Programme
- **First integrated air drop test** completed on 24 August 2025
- Four IAF test pilots completed training at Russia's Gagarin Cosmonaut Training Center (March 2021)
- Crew module designed for 3 people, ~400 km LEO, up to 7 days
- TV-D1 abort test was conducted on 21 October 2023 (already in our research)
- Crewed flight timeline still pending — no confirmed crewed launch date for 2026

### Shubhanshu Shukla — First Indian on ISS
- **Axiom Mission 4 (Ax-4), June 2025**: Shubhanshu Shukla flew as mission pilot to the ISS
- First Indian astronaut to visit the ISS
- Conducted ~60 experiments over approximately two weeks, including 7+ developed by ISRO/Indian institutions
- NOTE: Rakesh Sharma (1984) was first Indian in space (aboard Soviet Salyut 7), but Shukla is the first Indian on the ISS

### LVM3 — All 7 Missions Successful
- **CMS-03** (Communication satellite): Launched 2 November 2025, LVM3-M5
- **AST SpaceMobile BlueBird**: Launched 24 December 2025, LVM3-M6 — heaviest foreign satellite launched from India (6,100 kg)
- All seven LVM3 missions to date have achieved full success

### Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS)
- India's planned modular space station
- Target mass: ~20 tonnes
- Orbit: ~400 km altitude
- Crew capacity: 3, for 15–20 day stays initially
- Target: Fully operational by 2035
- Relies on docking technology demonstrated by SpaDeX

### Artemis Programme Updates
- **Artemis II** completed in April 2026 — first crewed Orion flight around the Moon
- Crew: NASA's Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch + CSA's Jeremy Hansen
- Set record for greatest distance humans have traveled from Earth (252,756 miles)
- **Artemis III** redesigned as LEO demonstration mission (not a lunar landing)
- **First lunar landing moved to Artemis IV** (targeted 2028)

### Tiangong Space Station
- **Shenzhou 23** launched May 2026 — first attempt at a year-long mission
- First Hong Kong resident (Lai Ka-ying) entered space on 25 May 2026
- Pakistani astronaut training underway, expected on Shenzhou 24 (October 2026)
- Plans to expand from 3 to 6 modules (~180 tonnes) by ~2027
- Xuntian space telescope planned for 2027 (2 m mirror, co-orbiting with Tiangong)

### ISRO Statistics (as of September 2026)
- 434 foreign satellites from 34 countries launched
- 136 spacecraft missions
- 106 launch missions
- 18 student satellites
- 9 re-entry missions
- 14,637 employees (as of March 2026)

### Second Spaceport
- SSLV Launch Complex at **Kulasekarapattinam, Tamil Nadu** — under construction
- Will be India's second launch facility (after SDSC SHAR, Sriharikota)
